About our project

Team: EcoDragons

Our focus: Pollinators and seeds. Bees, butterflies, moths, bats and birds carry pollen so plants can make seeds, and many animals also spread those seeds to new places. Together they help gardens and wild places grow and thrive.

The problem: Many people don't know how much we depend on pollinator animals, or that they are losing flowers, safe homes and clean air to live in.

Our idea: This awareness website. It lets anyone meet the pollinators, see what threatens them, take a quiz, and pick simple actions that help a garden glow.
